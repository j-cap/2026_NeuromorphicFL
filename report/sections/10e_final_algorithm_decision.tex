\subsection{Final standard FL form}

Q3 removes the largest ambiguity in the client-side input. The standard FL form uses the local model delta
\begin{equation}
    \Delta_i^r=w_{i,E}^r-w^r
\end{equation}
and updates the client communication state according to
\begin{equation}
    z_i^{r+1}
    =\rho z_i^r
    +\frac{p_i}{\eta}\Delta_i^r.
\end{equation}
A coordinate fires when its evidence magnitude crosses $\Delta$, transmits its coordinate address and sign, is fully reset, and the server applies the independently scheduled quantum $q_r$. The earlier gradient-driven asynchronous formulation remains a supported special case and an important mechanism-analysis setting, but it is no longer the defining FL interface.

The final systems interpretation additionally includes the Q2 synchronization layer: the server's ordered sparse update stream can be replayed exactly by clients, with a dense float32 checkpoint used whenever replay becomes more expensive than resynchronization. This makes the communication claim bidirectional rather than uplink-only.

\subsection{What the final baseline campaign establishes}

The publication-level baseline campaign uses the frozen Event-FedAvg semantics, a matched local optimizer, a development-only compression tuning budget for the comparator families, three independent held-out partitions, and symmetric end-to-end communication accounting.

On the MLP, Event-FedAvg has mean test CE 0.4784 and mean accuracy 83.14\% at 183.5~Mbit conservative bidirectional traffic. Quality-selected EF-TopK requires 1010.5~Mbit, quality-selected Strom 1610.9~Mbit, and dense FedAvg 2487.0~Mbit, while none improves the mean predictive result.

The CNN preserves an important negative qualification. Quality-selected Strom reaches 82.24\% mean accuracy versus 81.34\% for V1, but requires 359.5~Mbit versus 72.8~Mbit. Thus V1 does not uniformly dominate every high-traffic residual-pulse operating point. The traffic-matched control is more directly relevant to the Q1 mechanism distinction: Strom at $\tau=0.02$ uses 82.0~Mbit and reaches 79.88\% on the CNN, while V1 uses 72.8~Mbit and reaches 81.34\%. On the MLP, Strom and V1 are almost exactly traffic matched at 181.0 and 183.5~Mbit, while V1 improves mean accuracy from 79.51\% to 83.14\%.

The correct final empirical claim is therefore a communication--performance claim, not uniform predictive dominance:
\begin{center}
\resizebox{0.96\linewidth}{!}{$\displaystyle \boxed{
\text{At comparable communication, V1 provides a stronger predictive operating point than its closest tested residual-pulse sibling.}
}$}
\end{center}
A tuned Strom configuration can buy slightly better CNN accuracy only by moving substantially toward the high-traffic side of the frontier.

\subsection{Remaining tasks are theory and manuscript closure}

The core algorithmic and empirical consolidation tasks are now complete. The highest-priority remaining work is:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item derive the strongest appropriate mathematical characterization of the frozen lossy stateful compressor, preferably a convergence, perturbation, or compression-error result that respects full reset and the independent $q_r$ schedule rather than reintroducing the rejected hard-descent machinery;
    \item use that analysis to identify only genuinely necessary additional reviewer-facing checks, if any, instead of reopening broad mechanism exploration;
    \item create a separate manuscript LaTeX root and extract the concise problem--method--analysis--experiment story from this living report.
\end{enumerate}

Further datasets, larger CNNs, adaptive resolution, clustering, personalization, filter/block events, and larger client fleets remain optional strengthening directions. None is currently required to determine what the algorithm is or whether the communication mechanism is empirically viable.

The project has now passed the transition criterion from discovery to paper construction. The living report remains the single source of truth while the theory is developed. Once the theoretical statement and assumptions are frozen, a separate manuscript can be created without creating a second evolving research record.
