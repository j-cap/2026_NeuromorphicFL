# IJCNN 2027 author and submission attestations

Complete this checklist immediately before the regular-paper submission. Do
not commit author emails, conflicts, or other private submission metadata to
the public repository.

## Authors

- [ ] The author list, order, affiliations, and corresponding author are final.
- [ ] Every listed author satisfies the IEEE authorship criteria and approves
      the manuscript, results, disclosures, and submission.
- [ ] Every author has supplied any identifier required by the live IJCNN 2027
      submission form.

## Publication ethics

- [ ] This manuscript is original and is not under review at another venue.
- [ ] Related manuscripts, preprints, reports, theses, and prior conference
      papers have been disclosed to the submitting author and handled according
      to the live IJCNN 2027 and IEEE rules.
- [ ] All text, figures, tables, equations, code, citations, and numerical
      claims have been checked by the authors.
- [ ] The acknowledgment accurately identifies the AI systems used, the parts
      of the work they assisted, and the level of assistance.

## Anonymous submission package

- [ ] The live IJCNN 2027 instructions still require double-blind review, and
      the PDF and submission metadata follow the stated anonymization rules.
- [ ] Self-citations use third-person wording where required.
- [ ] The source archive, PDF metadata, acknowledgments, filenames, and public
      artifact links do not reveal author identities beyond what the live rules
      explicitly allow.

## Final technical check

- [ ] The official 2027 instructions confirm the required page size and any
      rule about paid extra pages. The LaTeX class option matches that rule.
- [ ] `latexmk` and `check_submission_compliance.py` pass on the exact PDF.
- [ ] The submission portal or conference-provided IEEE PDF service accepts
      the exact PDF, and the uploaded rendering has been inspected page by page.
- [ ] The title, abstract, author list, keywords, track, conflicts, and other
      portal metadata match the approved manuscript.
- [ ] At least one author can present the accepted paper and has confirmed the
      applicable registration obligation.
